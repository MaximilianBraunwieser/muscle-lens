MuscleLens

Aim the circle at any part of a body (a leg, an arm, the chest or the back) and see the muscle underneath.

  1. Close-ups work: the whole body doesn't need to be in frame.
  2. The muscle under the circle is highlighted and explained.
  3. Tap any other muscle to look at it instead.
Everything runs on your phone. No video leaves the device.
